## Approach

2. `src/core/mod.rs`: `Error::{unknown_relation, relation_in_use, relation_label_taken, forget_blocked_by_relations}`; move `is_used` out of `types.rs` to be shared.
3. `src/core/relation.rs` (new): `RelationKind`, `Page`, `valid_name`/`check_name`, `resolve`/`resolve_iri`, `kinds()` (one fixed SPARQL query over the union graph plus the four), `add`, `list`, `remove` (the `types::remove` skeleton), `relate`, `unrelate`, shared fixed queries for find, show and forget. Make the needed `resource.rs` helpers `pub(crate)`; do not duplicate.
4. `src/core/resource.rs`: `Shown.relations`, `Query.{related_to, via}`, `forget(ctx, store, id, force)`.
5. `src/cli/mod.rs`: `Relate`/`Unrelate`, `Command::Relation(Add|List|Remove)` through `vocabulary_mutated` with noun `relation`, `Forget{force}`, `find --related-to/--via` (`requires`), `show` text lines, the `--force` retry.
6. Schemas: `relate.json`, `unrelate.json`, `relation_add.json`, `relation_list.json`, `relation_remove.json`, `$defs`, `show.json`, `error.json` branches. `SCHEMAS` 17 → 22.

## Security call

No listed security-sensitive path is touched. Security-reviewer recommended: `forget --force` widens a destructive command; `relation remove` reads stdin; two new embedded SPARQL constraints reach rudof's evaluator (can reach the network through `SERVICE`); user references must reach SPARQL only through `substitute_variable`. Covered by byte comparisons, `IN_USE` over every graph with blank nodes, standard kinds refused, `add_used_iri_is_usage`, `printable`, and the TTY tests.

## Risks

rudof property paths (`*`/`+`) inside `sh:sparql` are untested here (SHACL fixtures pin them); zero-length `rdfs:subPropertyOf*` with a bound end (pinned by `subproperty_path_over_fixture`); one more per-Resource SPARQL constraint on every mutation (#48); a hand-edited dangling relation makes every mutation fail SHACL until `unrelate` clears it; `shell_quote` quotes ids with `:` in suggested commands; the new `forget` arity breaks settled call sites until updated in the same commit.
